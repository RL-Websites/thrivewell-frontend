Flow by ThriveWell RX

Pharmacy Workflow & Fulfillment Management System

Flow is ThriveWell RX's pharmacy-focused operations platform designed to help 503A pharmacies and fulfillment teams manage prescription workflows, order tracking, reporting, and integrations from one secure system.
Flow supports:

  • Prescription intake management
  • Pharmacy fulfillment tracking
  • Clinic and partner setup
  • Sales representative portal
  • Secure cloud-based access
  • Mobile-friendly operations
  • On-demand reporting
  • API integrations

Flow helps pharmacies streamline daily operations, improve visibility, and support high-volume fulfillment workflows with greater control.
